The event

What we run

Claude Builder Hacks is McGill's premier AI hackathon. Over 24 hours, 250+ students form teams to build and ship real working projects with frontier AI, guided by mentors, fuelled by workshops, and judged by people who build for a living.

What is a hackathon?

A time-boxed build sprint. Teams of two to four form on the first morning, pick a problem, and have until final demos to ship something that runs. Mentors circulate throughout; judges see live software, not pitches.

For students it is the fastest route from an idea to a working prototype. For sponsors it is the clearest view you will get of how a cohort of builders actually works.
